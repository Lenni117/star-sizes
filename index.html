    Reading the values

    measuredDirectly observed, e.g. an angular diameter converted with a distance. derivedCalculated from other values (for example L from R and T). spectroscopicFitted to the star’s spectrum. model fitResult of a model-atmosphere fit. literatureTaken from a published paper. estimateA rough range from typical stellar lifetimes. Not part of any data source. referenceNominal or textbook value.

    Sizes are drawn to a common scale. Catalogues that use different methods are never merged: every star shows the source of its own numbers.

    Sources

    • Red supergiant KY Cyg: Levesque et al. 2005, model-atmosphere fits.
    • Yellow hypergiant RW Cep: 2026 survey, angular diameter and Gaia group distance.
    • Named stars: JMDC measured angular diameters (Duvert 2016) with distances from the HYG database v4.1 (CC BY-SA 4.0). Temperatures come from B−V colour and are least reliable for hot stars.
    • O stars: IACOB spectroscopy (Holgado et al. 2018). Radius and luminosity derived with HYG distances.
    • White dwarfs: Gaia EDR3 catalogue, Gentile Fusillo et al. 2021. Radius from mass and surface gravity.
    • Sun and Earth: nominal values.
    • Ages: typical lifetimes for each kind of star, shown as estimates.
